Released November 2026. Copyright Takin.solutions and Swiss Art Research Infrastructure (SARI), University of Zurich. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

CRM_AAA_v1.5.4.2.rdf corrects the RDFS serialization of CRMaaa 1.5.4. It is derived from CRM_AAA_v1.5.4.1.rdf; the model is that of the 1.5.4 specification, which is unchanged. Namespace: https://takin.solutions/ontologies/crmaaa/

## Corrected identifiers

Four IRIs were misspelled or did not match their label and the specification. They are corrected in the IRI itself; data using the old IRIs should be updated.

| Term | 1.5.4.1 | 1.5.4.2 |
|---|---|---|
| ZP27 | ZP27_acribes_residence_place | ZP27_ascribes_residence_place |
| ZP36i | ZP36i_referent_ascribed_by | ZP36i_is_referent_ascribed_by |
| ZP59i | ZP59i_is_oglibation_relation_ascribed_by | ZP59i_is_obligation_relation_ascribed_by |
| ZP64i | ZP64i_is_property_subject_of | ZP64i_is_property_right_subject_of |

## Added

- owl:inverseOf declarations for all 71 pairs of properties and their inverses (142 statements).
- rdfs:range ZE6 Social Status for ZP19i is social status relation ascribed by, which had none.
- crm:E13_Attribute_Assignment rdfs:subClassOf ZE13 Speech Act, as in the class hierarchy of the specification.
- A licence statement (dcterms:license, CC BY-SA 4.0) and version information on the ontology resource.

## Corrected

- ZP26 has residence subject: its superproperty was declared as the class ZE1 Institutional Fact; it is now ZP1 has intentional subject.
- ZP62i surrendered property right through: superproperty corrected from P14 carried out by to P14i performed, matching its direction (from E39 Actor).
- Labels: ZP12i (is classification ascribed by), ZP27 (ascribes residence place), ZP30i (is relative ascribed by), ZP49i (is set belonging relation ascribed by), ZP51i (is current or former set member of), ZP68i (had obligation to generated by).
- Double spaces removed from the labels of ZP2i, ZP7i, ZP22i, ZP38i, ZP62i, ZP70i and ZP74i.

## Known differences from the specification document

The specification PDF has typos in the inverse names of ZP13i, ZP24i and ZP56i and in the name of ZP27; the serialization has the correct forms. The specification gives the inverse names of ZP69i and ZP70i differently; the serialization keeps the names of 1.5.4.1.
